# PaperGame

A top-down roguelike drawn on notebook paper: pick a class, fight through stages and bosses,
build your gear from the shop, and come back stronger with the Pencil Case.
Made with Phaser 3, runs offline, works on PC (keyboard, mouse, gamepad) and on phones (touch).

**Play:** https://thelucena06.github.io/PAPERGAME/

- 1 or 2 players on the same PC (keyboard + controller, or 2 controllers)
- On a phone: open the link, then "Add to Home Screen" to play offline later
- No install, no account

Offline / lab PCs: download this repo as a ZIP (Code → Download ZIP), unzip, open `index.html`.

Docs: `VISION.md` (the plan), `ROADMAP.md`, `CONTENT.md` (every item and change), `NOTES.md` (code notes).
